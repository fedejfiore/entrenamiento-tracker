Privacy policy

Effective October 2, 2026

This is a translation. If there is any discrepancy, the Spanish version prevails, except where the law of your country requires otherwise.

In short
  • Your workouts, measurements, photos and recordings are stored only on your phone or computer. We have no servers holding your data and no user accounts.
  • The app uses no advertising, analytics or trackers, and technically cannot connect to third-party services.
  • Data leaves your device only when you decide: a backup, an image you share or feedback you send us.
  • You can view, export and delete everything from the app at any time.

1. Who is responsible

Esō Agōn is an app by Inquieto (SFT division), based in the Argentine Republic. For any privacy question, write to federicojfiore@gmail.com.

2. What data is used and where it stays

DataPurposeWhere it is stored

Body measurements and other data about your health are sensitive data under Argentine Personal Data Protection Law 25,326 and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, art. 9). That is why they never leave your device and are not used for any other purpose.

Everything is optional: you can use the app without entering measurements, photos or contact details.

3. Device permissions

  • Microphone: only if you record your own voice alerts. Recordings stay on your device.
  • Photos and files: only when you pick an image or a backup. The app does not browse your gallery.
  • Notifications and alarms (Android app): to tell you when a rest period ends and remind you of your plan days. They are scheduled on the phone itself.
  • Keep screen on: so it doesn't turn off while you train, if you enable it.

4. When data leaves your device (always your decision)

  • Backups: the app creates a file that you save wherever you choose (for example, your Google Drive). You can protect it with a password (256-bit AES-GCM encryption). That file is under your control and subject to the terms of the service where you store it.
  • Shareable images: medals, records and summaries you choose to send through other apps.
  • Feedback and bug reports: sent from your own email app or WhatsApp. We receive what you write, your name and contact and, if you keep the option checked, technical details (app version, browser model, screen size, language and the last error, with none of your workout data). We use them only to reply and improve the app, and delete them when no longer needed.
  • Calendar and music: if you add the plan to your calendar or open a playlist, the chosen service opens (Google Calendar, Spotify, YouTube Music), under its own policies.

5. Hosting

The web version is published on GitHub Pages and the Android installer is downloaded from GitHub. Like any website, GitHub may log IP addresses and technical connection data to operate and protect its service, according to its privacy statement. We do not receive those logs.

6. Legal bases (GDPR)

For people in the European Union or the United Kingdom: app data is processed on your own device to provide the service you request (art. 6(1)(b)) and, for health data, with your explicit consent when you enter it (art. 9(2)(a)), which you can withdraw by deleting it. Feedback you send us is processed with your consent (art. 6(1)(a)).

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, update, delete and export your data, and to object to its processing (arts. 14–16 of Law 25,326; arts. 15–22 GDPR; and equivalent rights in other jurisdictions, such as the California Consumer Privacy Act).

  • Since your data lives on your device, you exercise them directly: Settings → Data: “Backup” to export it and “Reset everything” (or uninstall the app, or clear the site data in your browser) to delete it.
  • For feedback you sent us, write to federicojfiore@gmail.com and we will reply within 10 calendar days.

Under Law 25,326, you may exercise the right of access free of charge at intervals of no less than six months, unless a legitimate interest is shown (art. 14(3)). The Argentine Agency for Access to Public Information (AAIP), as the supervisory authority under Law 25,326, handles complaints from anyone whose data protection rights have been affected. If you are in the European Union, you may also lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

8. Security and retention

Data is kept on your device until you delete it. Its security also depends on your device: use a screen lock and make regular backups, because if you lose your phone without a backup we have no way of recovering your data.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in EU countries that require it), and we do not knowingly collect their data. People under 18 should use it with the permission and guidance of a parent or guardian, and train under adult supervision.

10. International transfers

The app does not transfer your data. If you email us, the message passes through your email provider's servers (for example, Google, in the United States).

11. Future features

Accounts, sync across devices, AI features or a paid plan may be added later. Before any of them uses your data outside your device, we will update this policy, explain it clearly and ask for your consent. We will never sell your personal data.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change it, we update the date above and, if the change is significant, we let you know in the app.
